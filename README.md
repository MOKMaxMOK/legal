# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application ("App") created by the developer. The App is a client-side utility designed to run locally on your device and interact directly with third-party AI services using your own credentials.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
The App **does not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information, user data, or telemetry to any backend server operated by the developer. 
* **Local Storage:** All app states, chat history, and configurations are stored locally on your device and remain entirely under your control.
* **No Developer Servers:** We do not operate any servers to process or log your activity.

### 2. Third-Party API Key and Data Transmission
The App utilizes a "Bring Your Own API Key" (BYOK) model. 
* When you use AI-powered features, your input prompts and data are transmitted **directly** from your device to the specific, third-party AI platform provider corresponding to the API key you provided (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, etc.).
* The developer of this App does not intercept, collect, or have access to your API keys or the data sent to and received from these third-party platforms. 
* Your use of those third-party AI services is governed by their respective terms of service and privacy policies.

### 3. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, it is inherently compliant with children's privacy regulations.

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
